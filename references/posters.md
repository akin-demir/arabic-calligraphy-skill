# Poster design

Choose a short headline, a readable subtitle and sufficient negative space. Use 1080x1080 for square, 1080x1350 for portrait and 1080x1920 for stories. Render and inspect each aspect ratio independently. Font size controls actual shaping; stretching a finished image distorts letters. Color and border are decoration, not evidence of classical authenticity. The built-in poster uses a measured centered headline and Naskh supporting text. It does not perform custom interweaving or geometric calligraphy.
