# Kufic — الخط الكوفي

Alternate names: Kufi.

## Background and visual features

A broad historical family of angular scripts, not a single fixed alphabet. Angular construction and strong horizontal rhythm.

## Proportions, joining and composition

Proportions depend on pen width, school and period. This reference does not prescribe a universal dot-count formula. Arabic joining classes remain meaningful; a font cannot reproduce every historical alternate or custom overlap. The implementation uses the font’s OpenType substitutions and positioning, with Pango shaping and bidirectional paragraph layout.

## Uses and readability

Contemporary display and wordmarks. Modern Kufi fonts do not reproduce all early manuscript forms.

## Fonts, licensing and rendering

Bundled family: Noto Kufi Arabic Regular. See [font licenses](../../FONT_LICENSES.md) for exact versions, hashes and OFL embedding permissions. Output is font-based typography, not expert-certified classical calligraphy.

## Frequent errors

Do not infer authenticity from a style name, distort letters to fit a canvas, reverse Unicode, or insert ornamental marks into the linguistic text. Modern Kufi fonts do not reproduce all early manuscript forms.

## Examples and references

Run `arabic-calligraphy render --style kufic --text "العلم نور" --output sample.svg`. Inspect marks, margins and reading order.
