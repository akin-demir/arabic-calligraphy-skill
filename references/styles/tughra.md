# Tughra — الطغراء

Alternate names: Tugra, Tuğra.

## Background and visual features

An Ottoman emblem and composition tradition rather than an independent alphabet. Intertwined names, vertical shafts and sweeping loops.

## Proportions, joining and composition

Proportions depend on pen width, school and period. This reference does not prescribe a universal dot-count formula. Arabic joining classes remain meaningful; a font cannot reproduce every historical alternate or custom overlap. Composition requires a qualified practitioner. No automatic joining or layout implementation for this tradition is supplied.

## Uses and readability

Expert-designed emblems. Emblem recognition may matter more than straightforward reading.

## Fonts, licensing and rendering

No suitable font has been vetted and integrated for this style. No automatic substitution is offered. A requested rendering fails explicitly. Font suitability, exact version, embedding and redistribution rights must be checked before adding one.

## Frequent errors

Do not infer authenticity from a style name, distort letters to fit a canvas, reverse Unicode, or insert ornamental marks into the linguistic text. Emblem recognition may matter more than straightforward reading.
